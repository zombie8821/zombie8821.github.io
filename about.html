Where I'm coming from

I'm a first-year Computer Science student who believes the best way to understand technology is to take it apart, build something with it, and keep asking why. My early work in C++ has pushed me to think carefully about logic, structure, and what happens beneath the surface.

Alongside school, I work part-time at Pikeville Medical Center. Balancing work, classes, and personal projects has taught me how to stay disciplined, communicate clearly, and keep moving when a problem takes longer than expected.

I'm especially interested in cybersecurity and machine learning—particularly how intelligent systems could help people recognize misinformation and make better decisions online. Right now, I'm focused on mastering the fundamentals that will let me contribute meaningfully to those problems.
